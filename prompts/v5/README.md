# TADAFFUQ — Upgrade Platform Pack v5

> **تَدَفُّق** — *flow* / *current* / *cascade*. The pack that destroys the classic dashboard and rebuilds the platform as a fluid, spatial, premium learning environment.

## What v5 is

A complete reset from the roots. v4 (ÊLAN) was an **additive doctrine** — Sacred Assets preserved, Cathedral chrome retained, Eight Worlds layered on top. v5 is **subtractive then re-creative**:

- The classic *Sidebar + Header + Main Content* tripartite is **demolished**.
- Navigation becomes **spatial** (a floating dock, not a wall).
- The canvas runs **edge-to-edge** with no fixed chrome.
- Bento cards are **alive** — they morph in place, never link out.
- Sub-detail panels **slide over** the canvas; they never become a new page.
- Active training enters a **Zen / Flow state** that strips everything but the workbench.
- Every interaction has **kinetic feedback** — spring scale on press, no popup toasts.
- **Zero emoji**, anywhere. Premium monochromatic SVG icons only.

The aesthetic is one coherent voice (not v4's eight-worlds polyphony):
**Deep-Space dark · Premium-Oatmeal light · Neon-Cyan progress · Electric-Orange action.**

## What v5 keeps

- The **14 sacred Upg.\* APIs** (state, theme, nav, cmdk, …) — same surface, new chrome.
- The **content** of the 12 ε page revivals from v4 — re-cased into the new layout.
- The **doctrine cadence** — manifesto + doctrines + atlas + AUTO_PILOT, exactly the v4 rhythm.

## What v5 abandons

- The classic sidebar, the fixed topbar, the centered max-width container.
- The Eight Worlds palette polyphony (one premium voice replaces eight cultural voices).
- Every emoji in markup (legacy or new).
- Modals, toasts, separate sub-pages, animated counters, "Welcome back, Name!" greetings.
- v4's preservation-first stance — v5 is allowed to delete legacy chrome wholesale, with one constraint: the public APIs stay.
